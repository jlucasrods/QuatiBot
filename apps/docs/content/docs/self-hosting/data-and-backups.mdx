---
title: "Data and backups"
description: "Persistent workspace and secret storage"
---

The workspace directory contains bots, transcripts, routines, memory, attachments and execution records. `QUATI_DATA_DIR` selects its location.

Workspace backups and exports are private data. Keep them on trusted storage and verify restoration in a disposable workspace before relying on them.

Portable workspace exports exclude credential vaults and keys. Back up these files separately with access controls appropriate for secrets. Encrypted credentials require their original key; unreadable vaults fail closed.

See [security](/docs/security) for the key files and permission boundaries.
